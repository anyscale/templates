The whole RL loop on one screen

Stage 0 ships your code to the worker. Then it cycles: sample a batch, render and generate four samples per prompt, score them with your own env code, normalise rewards within each group into advantages, recompute logprobs, apply the clipped surrogate loss, step AdamW, and broadcast the new weights back into the engines.
